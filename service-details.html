What We Inspect Across 200 Critical Points

We don't just kick tires. Our technicians utilize state-of-the-art digital paint meters, OEM diagnostic scanners, and engine pressure gauges.

Engine & Powertrain (45 Pts)
  • Cylinder compression & oil analysis
  • Turbocharger boost & wastegate actuation
  • Radiator pressure & coolant contamination
  • Transmission shifting smoothness & torque converter
  • Drive belts, tensioners, and motor mounts
Body & Structural Frame (40 Pts)
  • Digital paint depth metering for hidden bondo/repainting
  • Frame rail alignment & collision crumple zones
  • Flood waterlines & underbody salt corrosion
  • Panel gap tolerances & door latch hinges
  • Glass integrity, weatherstripping, and sunroof seals
Diagnostics & Electronics (45 Pts)
  • Full ECU/TCM computer scan for cleared trouble codes
  • Alternator charging output & battery health test
  • ADAS radar sensors, blind-spot, & cameras
  • HVAC heating & AC refrigerant temperature
  • Power windows, motorized seats, and infotainment
Brakes, Tires & Steering (40 Pts)
  • Caliper pad thickness & rotor runout measurement
  • Tire tread depth (32nds of an inch) & DOT date code
  • Shock absorbers, springs, and sway bar bushings
  • Tie rods, ball joints, and steering rack play
  • Wheel bearing hub noise & differential seals
Dynamic Road Test (30 Pts)
  • Cold-start cranking, idle stability, & vibration
  • Wide-open throttle acceleration & hesitation
  • High-speed braking straightness & ABS pulse
  • Cruising wheel alignment pull & road noise
  • 4WD / AWD transfer case engagement check
