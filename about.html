Our Story & Mission

Reinventing The Way Cars Are Bought & Sold

Founded on the simple belief that purchasing a used automobile should be as transparent, reliable, and stress-free as buying a brand new vehicle from the factory.

Why We Exist

Eliminating Asymmetric Information From The Used Car Market

For decades, car shopping meant dealing with hidden frame rust, undisclosed accidents, pushy dealership finance managers, and surprise fees.

AutoMarket was established to introduce radical transparency. Every listing on our platform includes an unedited, independent 200-point mechanical inspection, high-resolution diagnostic scanner readouts, upfront all-inclusive pricing, and a 7-day money-back guarantee.
