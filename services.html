Engineering Rigor

Inside Our 200-Point Inspection Standard

We don't just kick tires. Our licensed ASE technicians deploy specialized ultrasonic paint meters, OEM diagnostic scanners, and engine pressure gauges on every audit.

Powertrain & Engine (50 Pts)

Exhaustive internal combustion, hybrid battery, and transmission diagnostic evaluation.

  • Cylinder compression & spark health
  • Turbocharger boost & wastegate test
  • Coolant pressure & head gasket seal
  • Transmission shifting & clutch slippage
  • Drive belts, pulleys & motor mounts
Frame & Paint Depth (40 Pts)

Advanced ultrasonic paint thickness gauge checking for concealed structural damage or body filler.

  • Digital paint meter scanning (mils)
  • Chassis frame rail laser alignment
  • A/B/C pillar structural weld integrity
  • Undercarriage rust & salt corrosion
  • Weatherstripping & body panel gaps
OBD-II Electronics (40 Pts)

Live vehicle computer readout uncovering hidden trouble codes or reset emission check systems.

  • Full ECU/TCU stored fault code scan
  • Emissions readiness monitor validation
  • Alternator charging & 12V battery load
  • Airbag SRS, ABS & Traction control
  • Infotainment, cameras & radar sensors
Brakes & Road Test (70 Pts)

Physical micrometer measurements followed by a 20-minute highway and city dynamic road test.

  • Brake pad and rotor thickness (mm)
  • Tire tread depth (32nds) & date codes
  • Shock absorbers, struts & ball joints
  • Steering tracking, vibration & alignment
  • Emergency braking & ABS response
