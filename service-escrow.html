Total Security

Four Layers of Protection for Buyers & Sellers

Whether you are buying a $10,000 commuter car or a $150,000 exotic from an out-of-state private party, our escrow guarantees safety.

Title & Lien Search

We verify that the vehicle title is clean (no salvage/flood) and coordinate direct payoff with any existing bank lienholders.

Secure Fund Holding

The buyer wires funds into our bonded, FDIC-insured escrow account where funds remain locked and protected.

Delivery & Inspection

The buyer receives the vehicle and has an inspection period to confirm vehicle condition matches the description.

DMV Paperwork Transfer

We generate legal bills of sale, notarized power of attorney, and submit state DMV registration and title transfer.

Step-By-Step

The Escrow Transaction Workflow

01
Buyer & Seller Agree

Either party initiates escrow by entering the agreed sale price, VIN, and contact information.

02
Buyer Funds Escrow

Buyer transfers payment via wire or ACH. AutoMarket verifies funds and notifies seller to ship or hand over keys.

03
Vehicle Handover

The vehicle is shipped or delivered. The buyer tests and accepts the car within the agreed verification window.

04
Funds & Title Released

Payment is immediately disbursed to seller and AutoMarket processes official DMV title and license plates.
